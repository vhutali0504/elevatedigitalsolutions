# Trade mark brief: EDS / Elevate Digital Solutions

**The business:** a freelance web development partnership in Johannesburg, operating since 2026 and not a registered company. It builds websites, booking systems and online stores for South African businesses.

**What we want to protect:** EDS as the main brand, with "Elevate Digital Solutions" as the full name, plus a logo: a hexagon containing an upward chevron and the letters EDS (`docs/logo/eds-brand-board.svg`).

## Searches done so far

Free CIPC IP Online search, no login, classes 35 and 42, 4 November 2026. This was a preliminary check by the owner, not an official search.

**Not searched:** the logo as an image, other classes, and the company register.
